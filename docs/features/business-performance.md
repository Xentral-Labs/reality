# Activities Business data supply (spec 378)

Reality remains the authority. `business_performance.overview` retains the original full calculation as the independent diagnostic/reconciliation reader. The owner-only Business HTTP route reads stored `business_projection` observations and never rebuilds/enqueues on reads.

The existing internal `projections.refresh` job processes this model. New Business jobs receive the earliest timestamp among newly enqueued projection work; existing older FIFO work retains precedence. It replaces affected order/message contributions rather than adding event effects. Repeated deliveries converge by reading current Reality. Canonical commitment terms and `_open_work_rows` own quantity/readiness rules; the cache never authorizes booking. Simulator mail/ack and provider email Source creation now emit `source_record.stored` transactionally because no interpretation stage otherwise announces local acknowledgements/replies.

Order and correspondence detail tables are disposable, tenant/generation scoped. Indexed flags/direction/waiting and timestamp/opaque-ID ordering support server filtering and keyset pages. Cursors bind tenant, published generation and filter; a rebuild invalidates old cursors. Live incremental cohort membership can change between pages; pagination does not freeze business activity.

Rebuilds scan in bounded pages and persist phase/cursor/counters in an existing projection summary row. The prior published generation remains readable. Publication records the retained starting event sequence, then replays everything committed during the rebuild. Interleaved changes may be visible early in a build; they are never skipped at its checkpoint. Interrupted transactions do not publish partial contributions. The shared builder advisory lock and worker fencing apply. Old generations are deleted in bounded chunks, never together with Reality.

A row's next transition includes every outstanding due date's risk entry/overdue crossing, hourly receipt/completion entry/expiry. The scheduler discovers expired checkpoints and the worker processes only due rows. Goods-flow expiries also schedule upkeep. Empty/quiet companies do not enqueue a five-second refresh. The shared scheduler polls at five seconds; fleet fairness and worker backlog can exceed the provisional ten-second target and require measurement.

`processing` exposes available/delayed/state, processed/target sequence, completion, next clock moment and rebuild phase/row progress. Cold UI metrics are unavailable, rather than complete zero observations. Pending/rebuilding/failed data is explicitly delayed, and network failures retain the last snapshot. Upstream provider freshness remains unknown. Read acknowledgement, recorded reply and completed work are separate: completion remains unknown without evidence; outgoing evidence is not verified recipient delivery.

Migration `0146_business_performance` adds caches and source/detail indexes. Apply migration before new API/worker code. No startup migration, production deployment or actual company-data change is part of this feature. Drain unfinished Business projection jobs before downgrade; additive schema may be retained for code rollback.

Ancillary inventory/replenishment/goods/document observations retain existing readers in the worker. Their builder costs and canonical stock-history aggregation remain potential bottlenecks for large item histories. Large fan-out and rebuilds are bounded per job but may require many shared scheduler rounds. See spec 378 benchmark and quickstart for measured evidence and remaining acceptance.
