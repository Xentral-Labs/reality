# Research: Demo Contribution Portfolio

## Decision: Extend the canonical profile with dedicated bounded transactions

**Rationale**: Existing historical invoices were authored to demonstrate trends, settlement, and operations, not retained acquisition-cost coverage. Adding dedicated source-backed transactions keeps their original meaning intact and gives every complete contribution an exact evidence chain.

**Alternatives considered**:

- Retroactively assign costs to all historical invoices: rejected because their sources did not state a complete cost basis and this would turn absence into invented authority.
- Auto-review continuous Demo Data: rejected because the generator intentionally demonstrates missing-cost intake and would create a second implicit financial authority.
- Keep only fixture A: rejected because one outcome does not let a business evaluator compare margin patterns.

## Decision: Add five complete cases beside fixture A

**Rationale**: Fixture A plus five new cases yields six complete outcomes, enough to show healthy, low, negative, zero-selling-cost, and allocated-heavy patterns while keeping initialization bounded.

**Alternatives considered**:

- Three total complete cases: rejected as too narrow to demonstrate selling-cost variety.
- Cost every seeded invoice: rejected as unbounded, misleading, and unnecessary for the product proof.

## Decision: Reuse one acquisition lot and one inventory review for the five new sales

**Rationale**: One authored lot split into five exact issue members proves the same FIFO and commercial-match path with less duplicated setup. Pre-existing item movements remain supplier-owned within the review, and the portfolio lot is company-owned.

**Alternatives considered**:

- One item and inventory review per case: truthful but unnecessarily repetitive and slower to seed.
- Reuse fixture A's reviewed members: rejected because it would blur scenario boundaries and risk quantity reuse.

## Decision: Record selling costs through one multi-line supplier invoice

**Rationale**: Each source line can state the exact cost for one scenario and ordinary selling assignments can split it into direct and allocated parts. This preserves source authority and makes replay/count assertions bounded.

**Alternatives considered**:

- One supplier invoice per cost category: valid but noisier without improving traceability.
- Put selling costs directly in the contribution review: rejected because the review confirms scope; it does not author expense values.

## Decision: Increment the immutable profile version

**Rationale**: The canonical baseline's business content changes materially. Version 3 lets new setup runs identify the new portfolio while existing version-2 companies remain untouched.

**Alternatives considered**:

- Keep version 2: rejected because identical profile identity would describe different seeded content.
- Backfill existing companies: rejected because setup replay must never override later source controls or mutate established business evidence.
