# Quickstart: Supplier Confirmations, Minimum Quantities and Three-Way Match

1. On a supplier, state minimum 50 and multiple 12 for the bike. Ordering 30 shows the hint and offers 60.
2. Order 100 at 10.00. Record the supplier's confirmation: 90, a week later, at 10.50. The line shows both.
3. Receive 90 and invoice 90 at 10.50 through the guided invoice. The match shows the line as matched.
4. Order 20 more, cancel the line with a reason, and record the supplier's charge of 40.00 against it. No finding is raised, and the line shows the cancellation and the charge.
5. Receive 5 short on another line. The match names `received_short`.
