# Quickstart: Serving Backorders on Receipt

1. Seed an item at a location, two customer promises (3 due later, 3 due earlier) and a purchase of 4 assigned to the later one.
2. Receive 4 against the purchase in the web; the result offers "Serve backorders".
3. Review: the assigned promise is proposed 3 first, then the earlier-due promise 1. Change the second line to 0, confirm, and check that only 3 are reserved.
4. Read supply coverage for the purchase's customers: the assigned one shows 3 arrived.
5. Open the item in the warehouse: available-to-promise names the purchase and its date.
6. Cancel the assigned customer order: its assignment no longer counts, and "Serve backorders" no longer lists it.
