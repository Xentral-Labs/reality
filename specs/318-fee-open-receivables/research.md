# Research

Existing fee documents already have receivable control postings and immutable source links. Settlement positions already derive effective allocations and reversals; extend selection, not posting logic. Customer-borne payment-return fees are claims; company-borne fees remain expenses.

Separate the payment settlement context from adjustment eligibility: accepting cash must not enable discounts or write-offs. Fee aging uses only stated document maturity; do not resolve party terms. Dunning eligibility remains unchanged on all entrypoints.

Historical visibility changes finance projection contents; increment its version rather than rewriting ledger history. No schema or migration is needed.
