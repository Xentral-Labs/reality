## What Must Never Close

- [ ] CHK009 Is any promise with movement against it excluded whatever its date, and is that read through the correction-aware quantity so a voided shipment does not protect it? [Completeness, Spec §FR-003, Plan §What matches]
- [ ] CHK010 Is the risk named that a company recording movements outside Reality has promises that look untouched and are not? [Completeness, Plan §Review Risks]
- [ ] CHK011 Are dateless promises excluded, with the reason that they belong to a different rule entirely? [Clarity, Spec §FR-005]
- [ ] CHK012 Is it explicit that no Document, LedgerEntry or Movement is touched, and argued from the difference between a decision about the future and a claim about the past? [Clarity, Spec §FR-011, §Clarifications]
