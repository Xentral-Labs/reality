# Quickstart: Stock Count Sessions

1. Seed a location with 20 wheels and 8 lamps. Reserve 4 + 4 + 4 wheels for three orders, and block 2 lamps.
2. In the warehouse stock view of the location, open "Inventur" and count 9 wheels and 5 lamps.
   - The review shows book and counted quantities.
   - Wheels −11 and lamps −3; of the lamps, 1 comes from the block.
   - The three reservations are named.
3. Confirm. The adjustments link to the count, and "Reservierung übersteigt Bestand" names the reservations.
4. Count again with an earlier counting time after shipping a wheel, and check that only the difference at that time is posted.
