# Terminology review

User-approved exception to spec208: localize Exception(s) and Decision(s) only. Existing navigation, Home, Inspector types, action/search labels, history, catalog/rules, empty states and helper prompts use shared catalog keys; their callers and routes remain unchanged. Spanish operational references use incidencia consistently rather than alternating with excepción. Shipping-specific delivery exception labels remain natural and unchanged. Intentional UI examples follow.
