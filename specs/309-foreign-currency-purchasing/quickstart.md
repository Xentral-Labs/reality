# Quickstart: Foreign-Currency Purchasing

1. In the finance settings, check that the company currency is EUR.
2. Order 100 units from a supplier in USD, receive them, and post the invoice of 1,000 USD at a stated rate of 0.92.
   - The review shows 920.00 EUR.
   - The posting shows both amounts and the rate.
3. Pay 400 USD with 372.00 EUR.
   - The review shows a payment rate of 0.93 and a loss of 4.00 EUR.
   - The invoice keeps 600 USD and 552.00 EUR open.
4. Pay the remaining 600 USD with 546.00 EUR.
   - The review shows a gain of 6.00 EUR.
   - The invoice is closed in both currencies.
5. On the receipt's costing, accept the offered conversion basis from the invoice rate. The landed cost reads in EUR.
6. Try to change the company currency: it is refused because the company has postings.
