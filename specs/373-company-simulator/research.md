# Research

Existing Sandbox proposal tools are suitable for finite explicitly confirmed fixture actions. Quick lesson restrictions do not define independent Sandbox admission. The spec372 bridge verifies that proposal preparation leaves accepted quantities unchanged and retains exact approval receipts.

Read-only observer has tenant-scoped stock, active reservations, per-order fulfillment/amounts, commitment/source IDs and lineage checks. Independent expectations must be authored from world demand and accepted commands, never copied from observed totals.

Application clocks remain real. Future accelerated dispatch timestamps fail normal validation; ordinal scenario time belongs in the journal. Existing outbound delivery plans preserve dispatch destinations and per-package carrier events establish observed arrival; this operational slice deliberately does not claim that proof.

Finance research located sales_invoice_record, customer_payment_post and open_invoice_amount. A later financial slice must reconcile explicit source totals, allocations and posting groups. Original Shopify formats are not present, so native mapping is deferred.
