# Private Analytics Access Contract

Eligibility: active persisted account AND (active membership in selected company OR persisted platform administrator AND selected tenant is a nonarchived business company). A principal flag alone is insufficient. Eligibility never substitutes for authorship: every private artifact list, detail, mutation, deferred result and readable held proposal retains selected-company and exact-author identity.

API/tool routes and schemas remain unchanged. Memberless administrators see their own private library, initially empty if they have no reports. Ordinary nonmembers receive the existing membership-required list code; inaccessible details remain not found. Deferred admission/execution/collection use the same eligibility and retain existing error semantics.

Switcher/card labels: actual Owner or Member first; otherwise explicit authenticated admin identity produces Platform administrator access; otherwise No company membership. Do not infer admin from absent role. Existing owner-management controls are unchanged. New labels and explanatory copy are localized in en/de/nl/es.
