# Business Journey Guide

Ask what Reality can do, or browse all cataloged business situations. A status is a product-evidence statement, not a document status: **supported** is proven by executable evidence; **partial** proves only part; **recognition only** can identify related evidence; **missing** needs product capability; and **out of scope** is deliberate.

The complete capability guide is broader than the [Demo data guide](./demo-data), which lists only cases you can inspect immediately in the canonical demo company. Public questions are read-only, do not inspect a company and do not execute tools.
