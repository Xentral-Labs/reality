# Evaluate Reality as an ERP professional

Reality is strongest where an operation must remain explainable from its received source through
business evidence to current operational records. It preserves original source payloads, interprets
them into documents and lines where applicable, and derives commitments, reservations, movements and
ledger effects without turning a document status into a second operational authority.

## What is demonstrated today

The Business Journey Guide contains the detailed authority for individual capabilities. Executable
journeys demonstrate core order-to-cash and purchase-to-pay building blocks, including order
commitments, availability and reservation, partial shipment and receipt, customer and supplier
invoicing, payment allocation, returns, credits and operational exception detection. The public
advisor can explain these product capabilities, but it cannot inspect company data or execute a
business action.

Reality's differentiator is not a longer checklist of document screens. It is the traceable chain
from SourceRecord through Document or DocumentLine to Fact, Commitment, Reservation, Movement or
LedgerEntry wherever those stages apply. Received values remain received evidence; current open
quantities, balances and exceptions are derived from the records Reality holds.

## Important evaluation limits

The Journey Guide deliberately distinguishes supported, partial, recognition-only, missing and
out-of-scope scenarios. A supported primitive must not be presented as proof of a complete process.
In particular, the current catalog does not establish every ERP exception, every integration or a
complete migration from a legacy ERP. A clean positive three-way-match result is not yet proven; the
demonstrated behavior is the detection of specific supplier-invoice findings such as invoiced
quantity above received quantity, price variance and invoice before receipt.

Tool names prove only that a governed application command exists with its documented mode. They do
not by themselves prove automation, an end-to-end workflow or fields returned by that command.
Mutating actions require confirmation, while the public product advisor is read-only and sees no
company data.

For an evaluation, start with one real operating flow and its difficult exceptions. Ask the advisor
what is native, what needs confirmation or manual work, and what is not established. Then use the
linked Journey entries to verify each material conclusion.
