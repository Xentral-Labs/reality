# Ask Reality

Ask whether Reality supports a business situation. Answers are grounded in the published
[Business Journey Guide](./business-journeys), cite the matching journey IDs and state the evidence
level and limitations. This public assistant is read-only: it cannot inspect company data or execute
tools.

For account-specific operational questions, use the normal Reality Chat. To propose a missing
situation and vote on suggestions,
<a href="__APP_URL__/?route=settings&settingsView=personal#journey-suggestions">open Reality</a>.
