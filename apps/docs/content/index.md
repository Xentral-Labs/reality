## Start with one business question

Which orders are still open? What stock is available? Which invoices remain unpaid? Reality lets
people and agents answer these questions and inspect the records behind the answers.

Start with [your starting recipe](/getting-started/), then copy the
[first-question prompt](/getting-started/first-question). You can use an external agent or the
configured Reality Chat.

## Explore when you need more

- [Demo examples](/getting-started/demo-data): find a concrete order, delivery or payment to
  inspect.
- [Guided storylines](/storylines/): play a business process step by step.
- [The business model](/concepts/business-reality-guide): understand **Source → Evidence →
  Reality**.
- [Supported business scenarios](/getting-started/business-journeys): check whether Reality covers
  your case.
- [Agent playbooks](/agent-playbooks/): work through sales, purchasing, warehouse and finance tasks.

For your own business, follow [the existing-company recipe](/getting-started/existing-business).
