## What Reality is

Reality is an open reference core for Business Reality and Agent Operations. It gives ERP
consultants, architects, process owners, and integration specialists an understandable, executable
example of how ERP data, operational truth, agent tools, human approval, and verifiable decisions
fit together. It does not replace every upstream tool.

Its primary learners are ERP professionals becoming **Process Owners**. They must define which
claims have evidence, which tools an agent may use, when human consent is required, and how an
effect is verified. The Head of Operations remains the primary product role in this reference case.

> **Current behavior:** Reality ships an Operations Cockpit, a Business Reality Inspector, source
> ingestion, tenant-scoped application interfaces, and traceable operational and financial records.

> **Normative invariant:** Every business flow preserves **Source → Evidence → Reality** wherever
> those stages apply. Product surfaces may simplify the view, but they never create a second version
> of business truth.

## Three areas you will find in the app

- **Business Graph:** How is it connected, and what happened? See related records as a graph and
  over time.
- **Business Facts:** What is recorded? Inspect individual records, their details, relationships and
  evidence.
- **Tools:** What can I query, calculate or execute? Find actions and commands, plus read-only views
  and projections.

These names stay the same in every language. [Your first product journey](/getting-started/) shows
how to use all three for one business case.

## Three learning paths

These times are approximate learning time. Installation and a production ERP connection are not
included.

- **15 minutes — understand the model:** Read
  [Foundations](/concepts/business-reality-guide/01-from-erp-documents-to-business-reality) and
  answer its exercise.
- **30 minutes — decide as a Process Owner:** Read
  [The Process Owner role](/concepts/business-reality-guide/04-working-as-process-owner), then
  [trace a result in the product](/getting-started/), or play a [storyline](/storylines/) and watch
  the rules at work.
- **60 minutes — prepare an ERP pilot:** Start with
  [What can be adapted?](/integrations/customization), then work through the
  [order example](/integrations/order-example).

## What open source means here

The model is not only described; it is inspectable. Readers can trace each central business claim
from the worked example through an executable catalog to its service and test. The core stays small
on purpose: it proves and teaches the shortest true relationships instead of pretending to cover
every ERP feature. Reality is available under the [MIT License](/reference/license).
